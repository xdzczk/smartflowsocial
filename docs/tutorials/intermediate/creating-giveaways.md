# Creating Giveaways

**Estimated time:** 30 minutes  
**Difficulty:** Intermediate  
**Last updated:** 2026-01-02

Learn how to create and manage Nostr giveaways with fair winner selection and engagement tracking.

---

## Prerequisites

- ✅ Account created and logged in
- ✅ Posted content with engagement
- ✅ Understanding of engagement types (likes, reposts, comments, zaps)
- ✅ Free plan or higher (giveaways available on all plans)

---

## What are Giveaways?

**Giveaways allow you to:**
- Track engagement on your posts
- Select random winners fairly
- Filter participants by engagement type
- Post results automatically

**Engagement types tracked:**
- **Reposts** (Kind 6) - Sharing your content
- **Likes** (Kind 7, 9735, 9734) - Positive reactions
- **Comments** (Kind 1) - Replies to your posts
- **Zaps** (Kind 9735) - Lightning payments

---

## Step 1: Create Engaging Content

**Before creating a giveaway:**

1. **Post content** to Nostr
   - Memes, announcements, updates
   - Anything that gets engagement
2. **Wait for engagement:**
   - Likes, reposts, comments, zaps
   - More engagement = more participants
3. **Content should be:**
   - Interesting and shareable
   - Clear about giveaway rules
   - Posted with relevant hashtags

**Example post:**
```
🎉 GIVEAWAY TIME! 🎉

Share this post, like, comment, or zap to enter!

3 random winners will get 2,000 sats each!

Ends in 7 days.

#bitcoin #lightning #giveaway #nostr
```

---

## Step 2: Navigate to Giveaways

1. **Click "Giveaways"** in navigation
   - Or go to `/dashboard/giveaways`
2. **Giveaways page** opens
3. **See posts with engagement**

---

## Step 3: Select a Post

1. **View posts with engagement:**
   - Sorted by total engagement
   - Shows engagement statistics
   - Filter by queue or time period

2. **Click on a post:**
   - Post details open
   - See engagement breakdown:
     - Likes count
     - Reposts count
     - Comments count
     - Zaps count and total sats

---

## Step 4: Configure Giveaway Period

1. **Select time period:**
   - **Last 7 days** (default)
   - **Last 14 days**
   - **Last 30 days**
   - **Custom range**

2. **Filter by queue:**
   - **All queues** - All posts
   - **Specific queue** - Posts from one queue
   - **Unassigned** - Posts without queue

3. **View participants:**
   - See all users who engaged
   - Filter by engagement type

---

## Step 5: Filter Participants

**Filter options:**

1. **By engagement type:**
   - **All** - All engagement types
   - **Reposts** - Only reposters
   - **Likes** - Only likers
   - **Comments** - Only commenters
   - **Zaps** - Only zappers

2. **By time period:**
   - Last 7/14/30 days
   - Custom date range

3. **Blacklist:**
   - Excluded accounts (configured in settings)
   - Automatically filtered out

---

## Step 6: Select Winners

**Random winner selection:**

1. **Click "Select Winners"** or similar
2. **System randomly selects:**
   - 3 random reposters
   - 3 random likers
   - 3 random commenters

3. **Winners displayed:**
   - See selected winners
   - View their profiles (npub)
   - Copy winner list

**Selection process:**
- Fair and random
- No bias or favoritism
- Transparent process
- Verifiable results

---

## Step 7: Generate Giveaway Post

1. **Click "Generate Message"** or similar
2. **System generates post:**
   ```
   Thanks to everyone who shared, zapped, liked and commented on our memes lately 🫡

   ✅ 3 random reposts from last week each got 2,000 sats - congrats to @npub1... 🎉

   ✅ 3 random reactions from last week each got 1,000 sats - congrats to @npub2... 🎉

   ✅ 3 random comments from last week each got 1,000 sats - congrats to @npub3... 🎉

   Enjoy your memes and have a nice day!

   #bitcoin #plebchain #giveaway
   ```

3. **Review generated post:**
   - Check winner npubs
   - Verify prize amounts
   - Ensure message is clear

---

## Step 8: Customize Message (Optional)

**Edit generated message:**

1. **Modify text:**
   - Add personal touches
   - Customize tone
   - Add additional content

2. **Adjust format:**
   - Change structure
   - Add emojis
   - Modify hashtags

3. **Keep important info:**
   - Winner npubs
   - Prize amounts
   - Time period

---

## Step 9: Post Results

**Option 1: Post Immediately**

1. **Copy generated message**
2. **Go to "New Post"**
3. **Paste message**
4. **Post immediately**

**Option 2: Schedule Post**

1. **Copy generated message**
2. **Go to "New Post"**
3. **Paste message**
4. **Schedule for later**

**Best practice:**
- Post results promptly
- Thank all participants
- Be transparent about winners

## Managing Blacklists

### Add to Blacklist

1. **Go to Settings → Engagement**
2. **Find "Giveaway Blacklist"**
3. **Add npub addresses:**
   - One per line
   - Format: `npub1...`
4. **Save blacklist**

**Use cases:**
- Exclude bots
- Exclude spam accounts
- Exclude specific users
- Maintain giveaway integrity

### Remove from Blacklist

1. **Go to Settings → Engagement**
2. **Find "Giveaway Blacklist"**
3. **Remove npub addresses**
4. **Save blacklist**

## Giveaway Best Practices

### Content Strategy

1. **Create engaging content:**
   - Memes, announcements, updates
   - Clear and shareable
   - Relevant to your audience

2. **Announce in advance:**
   - Post giveaway announcement
   - Include rules and timeline
   - Build anticipation

3. **Track engagement:**
   - Monitor participation
   - Check engagement metrics
   - Adjust strategy as needed

### Winner Selection

1. **Be transparent:**
   - Use random selection
   - Show selection process
   - Post results publicly

2. **Select fairly:**
   - No bias or favoritism
   - Random from all participants
   - Respect blacklist settings

3. **Post results promptly:**
   - Announce winners quickly
   - Include prize details
   - Thank all participants

### Prize Distribution

**Common patterns:**
- **Equal prizes** - Same amount for all winners
- **Tiered prizes** - Different amounts by category
- **Weighted selection** - More winners from higher engagement

**Example:**
- Reposters: 2,000 sats each (3 winners)
- Likers: 1,000 sats each (3 winners)
- Commenters: 1,000 sats each (3 winners)

## Troubleshooting

### No Engagement Shown

**Problem:** Post doesn't show engagement

**Solutions:**
- Check post on Nostr directly
- Verify post was published
- Wait for engagement to sync
- Check time period settings

### Winners Not Showing

**Problem:** No winners selected

**Solutions:**
- Check engagement statistics
- Verify participants exist
- Review blacklist settings
- Adjust filters

### Generated Message Issues

**Problem:** Message format incorrect

**Solutions:**
- Regenerate message
- Check winner data
- Verify npub format
- Try again
