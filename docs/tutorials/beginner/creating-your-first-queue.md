# Creating Your First Queue

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes  
**Difficulty:** Beginner  
**Last updated:** 2026-01-02

Learn how to create and configure queues to organize your posts and automate scheduling.

---

## Prerequisites

- ✅ Account created and logged in
- ✅ Completed [Creating and Scheduling Posts](./creating-and-scheduling-posts.md)
- ✅ Basic plan or higher (queues available on all paid plans)

---

## What is a Queue?

**A queue is a way to:**

- Organize posts by topic or type
- Automate scheduling with rules
- Maintain consistent posting times
- Group related content together

**Example queues:**

- "Bitcoin News" - Daily Bitcoin updates
- "Memes" - Fun content
- "Product Updates" - Company announcements
- "Educational" - Tutorials and guides

---

## Step 1: Navigate to Settings

1. **Click "Settings"** in navigation
2. **Go to "Content" tab** (or "Queues" section)
3. **Click "Queues"** section
4. **Queue management page** opens

---

## Step 2: Create New Queue

1. **Click "Create Queue"** or "New Queue" button
2. **Queue creation form** appears

---

## Step 3: Configure Basic Settings

**Fill in basic information:**

1. **Name:**

   - Enter descriptive name
   - Example: "Bitcoin News"
   - This appears in dropdowns

2. **Description (Optional):**

   - Add description
   - Example: "Daily Bitcoin and Lightning news"
   - Helps remember queue purpose

3. **Color:**
   - Choose color for calendar view
   - Helps visually distinguish queues
   - Example: Blue for news, Green for memes

---

## Step 4: Choose Schedule Type

**Two schedule types available:**

### Option 1: Interval Scheduling

**Best for:** Regular, consistent posting

**How it works:**

- Posts every X hours
- Calculated from last post time
- Example: Every 4 hours

**Configuration:**

1. **Select "Interval"**
2. **Set interval:**
   - Hours between posts
   - Example: 4 hours
3. **Set start time (optional):**
   - When first post should be
   - Example: 9:00 AM

**Example:**

- Interval: 4 hours
- First post: 9:00 AM
- Next posts: 1:00 PM, 5:00 PM, 9:00 PM

### Option 2: Fixed Slots

**Best for:** Specific posting times

**How it works:**

- Posts at exact times daily
- Example: 9am, 2pm, 7pm

**Configuration:**

1. **Select "Fixed Slots"**
2. **Add time slots:**
   - Click "Add Slot"
   - Enter time (e.g., 9:00 AM)
   - Repeat for each slot
3. **Remove slots:**
   - Click "Remove" on slot

**Example:**

- Slot 1: 9:00 AM
- Slot 2: 2:00 PM
- Slot 3: 7:00 PM

**For this tutorial, choose "Interval" with 4 hours.**

---

## Step 5: Assign Hashtag Preset (Optional)

**If you have hashtag presets:**

1. **Select "Default Hashtag Preset"**
2. **Choose preset** from dropdown
3. **Preset automatically applied** to posts in this queue

**Benefits:**

- Consistent hashtags
- Saves time
- Better organization

**For this tutorial, leave as "None".**

---

## Step 6: Save Queue

1. **Review settings:**

   - Name, description, color
   - Schedule type and rules
   - Hashtag preset

2. **Click "Save"** or "Create Queue"

3. **Queue created!**
   - Appears in queue list
   - Available in post creation
   - Ready to use

---

## Using Your Queue

### Assign Post to Queue

1. **Create new post**
2. **Select queue** from dropdown
3. **Queue rules apply:**
   - Scheduled time calculated
   - Hashtag preset applied (if set)
   - Post organized in queue

### View Queue Posts

1. **Go to Dashboard**
2. **Filter by queue:**
   - Select queue from filter
   - See all posts in queue
   - View scheduled times

### Calendar View

1. **Go to Calendar**
2. **See queue posts:**
   - Color-coded by queue
   - Visual representation
   - Easy to spot gaps

---

## Queue Management

### Edit Queue

1. **Go to Settings → Queues**
2. **Find queue** in list
3. **Click "Edit"**
4. **Modify settings:**
   - Change name, description
   - Update schedule rules
   - Change hashtag preset
5. **Save changes**

### Delete Queue

1. **Go to Settings → Queues**
2. **Find queue** in list
3. **Click "Delete"**
4. **Confirm deletion**

**Note:** Posts in deleted queue become "Unassigned"

### Set Default Queue

1. **Go to Settings → Queues**
2. **Find queue** to set as default
3. **Click "Set as Default"**
4. **Queue is default** for new posts

## Queue Best Practices

### Naming Conventions

**Use clear, descriptive names:**

- "Bitcoin News" ✅
- "Memes" ✅
- "Product Updates" ✅
- "Queue 1" ❌ (too vague)

### Organization

**Group by:**

- **Topic** - Bitcoin, Lightning, Nostr
- **Content Type** - News, Memes, Educational
- **Frequency** - Daily, Weekly, Monthly
- **Purpose** - Marketing, Support, Updates

### Schedule Planning

**Consider:**

- **Audience activity** - When are they online?
- **Content type** - News vs. evergreen
- **Frequency** - How often to post?
- **Time zones** - Where is your audience?

### Multiple Queues

**Create queues for:**

- Different topics
- Different posting frequencies
- Different content types
- Different campaigns

## Troubleshooting

### Queue Not Appearing

**Problem:** Queue created but not in dropdown

**Solutions:**

- Refresh page
- Check queue is enabled
- Verify plan includes queues
- Check queue settings

### Schedule Not Working

**Problem:** Posts not scheduling correctly

**Solutions:**

- Check schedule rules
- Verify timezone settings
- Review queue configuration
- Check for conflicts

### Can't Create Queue

**Problem:** "Create Queue" button disabled

**Solutions:**

- Check plan includes queues
- Verify account status
- Check queue limits
- Contact support
