# Using the Calendar View

**Estimated time:** 10 minutes  
**Difficulty:** Beginner  
**Last updated:** 2026-01-02

Learn how to use the visual calendar interface to schedule and manage your posts with drag-and-drop functionality.

---

## Prerequisites

- ✅ Account created and logged in
- ✅ Completed [Scheduling Posts](./scheduling-posts.md)
- ✅ Have at least one scheduled post

---

## What is the Calendar View?

**The calendar view provides:**

- Visual representation of scheduled posts
- Drag-and-drop rescheduling
- Weekly and monthly views
- Color-coded queue organization
- Easy date navigation

**Benefits:**

- See your schedule at a glance
- Quickly reschedule posts
- Identify gaps in your schedule
- Organize content visually

---

## Step 1: Navigate to Calendar

1. **Click "Calendar"** in navigation
   - Or go to `/dashboard/calendar`
2. **Calendar page** opens
3. **Default view:** Weekly view

---

## Step 2: Understand the Interface

**Calendar header:**

- **Date range** - Current week/month displayed
- **Navigation buttons** - Previous/Next, Today
- **View toggle** - Switch between Week and Month
- **Queue filter** - Filter by queue type (optional)

**Calendar body:**

- **Weekly view:** Horizontal timeline with time slots
- **Monthly view:** Calendar grid with post cards
- **Post blocks:** Visual representation of scheduled posts

---

## Step 3: Switch Between Views

**Weekly View:**

1. **Click "Week"** button in header
2. **See horizontal timeline:**
   - Days of week (Mon-Sun)
   - Hourly time slots (00:00-23:59)
   - Current time indicator (red line)
   - Post blocks positioned by time

**Monthly View:**

1. **Click "Month"** button in header
2. **See calendar grid:**
   - Days of the month
   - Post cards under each date
   - Color-coded by queue

**Switch anytime:**

- Toggle between views
- Posts remain visible
- Layout adapts to view

---

## Step 4: Navigate Dates

**Previous/Next:**

1. **Click "<"** to go back
2. **Click ">"** to go forward
3. **Moves by week** (weekly view) or **month** (monthly view)

**Jump to Today:**

1. **Click "Today"** button
2. **Calendar jumps** to current date
3. **Highlights today** in view

**Date range display:**

- Shows current week/month range
- Helps orient yourself
- Updates as you navigate

---

## Step 5: View Scheduled Posts

**In Weekly View:**

- Posts appear as blocks in time slots
- Positioned by scheduled time
- Shows post preview and time
- Color-coded by queue

**In Monthly View:**

- Posts appear as cards under dates
- Grouped by day
- Shows post preview
- Color-coded by queue

**Post information:**

- Preview of content
- Scheduled time
- Queue type (by color)
- Post type icon

---

## Step 6: Reschedule with Drag and Drop

**Simple reschedule:**

1. **Click and hold** post block
2. **Drag to new time slot:**
   - Weekly view: Drag to different time
   - Monthly view: Drag to different date
3. **Release to drop**
4. **Confirmation modal appears:**
   - Shows new scheduled time
   - Review changes
5. **Click "Confirm"**
6. **Re-sign event:**
   - Extension popup appears
   - Sign with new timestamp
7. **Post rescheduled!**

**Visual feedback:**

- Post follows cursor while dragging
- Drop zone highlights
- Modal confirms changes

---

## Step 7: Edit Post from Calendar

1. **Click on post block**
2. **Edit modal opens:**
   - View full post content
   - Edit post details
   - Change scheduled time
   - Modify queue
3. **Make changes**
4. **Save changes**
5. **Re-sign if time changed**

---

## Filtering by Queue

**To view specific queue:**

1. **Click queue filter** (if available)
2. **Select queue** from dropdown
3. **Calendar shows** only posts from that queue
4. **Color coding** helps identify queues

**To view all queues:**

1. **Select "All"** or clear filter
2. **All posts** appear
3. **Color coding** distinguishes queues

---

## Calendar Best Practices

### Visual Organization

1. **Use queue colors:**

   - Assign distinct colors to queues
   - Easy visual identification
   - Better organization

2. **Balance schedule:**

   - Don't overload time slots
   - Space posts appropriately
   - Consider audience timezone

3. **Group related content:**
   - Schedule similar posts together
   - Create content blocks
   - Maintain consistency

### Time Management

1. **Peak engagement times:**

   - Schedule during active hours
   - Use analytics to find best times
   - Test different schedules

2. **Consistency:**

   - Post at regular intervals
   - Use queues for automation
   - Maintain schedule

3. **Flexibility:**
   - Leave room for adjustments
   - Use calendar for quick changes
   - Don't over-schedule

## Troubleshooting

### Calendar Not Loading

**Problem:** Calendar view is blank

**Solutions:**

- Refresh the page
- Check you have scheduled posts
- Verify queue filter settings
- Clear browser cache

### Drag and Drop Not Working

**Problem:** Can't drag posts

**Solutions:**

- Check browser compatibility
- Disable browser extensions
- Try different browser
- Check JavaScript errors

### Posts Not Rescheduling

**Problem:** Reschedule doesn't save

**Solutions:**

- Check post has signed event
- Verify new time is in future
- Re-sign event if needed
- Check network connection
